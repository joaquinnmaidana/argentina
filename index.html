Argentina

This is a page that tells you a little bit more about the most passionate country in the world!

Things that Argentina is known for:

  • Football
  • Steak
  • Music
  • Mate
  • Wine
  • Dulce de Leche
  • Tourism

Top 3 people most known from Argentina:

  1. Lionel Messi

    He is widely considered one of the greatest athletes in history, winning a record eight Ballon d'Or awards and leading Argentina to victory in the 2022 FIFA World Cup.

  2. Diego Maradona

    He achieved legendary status by single-handedly winning the 1986 World Cup for his country, remembered forever for his brilliant skill and cultural impact.

  3. Che Guevara

    Born in Rosario, Argentina, his face became an enduring global countercultural symbol of rebellion and revolution across the world.
